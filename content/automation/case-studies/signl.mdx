Signl is a fan-to-artist platform: artist pages, a merch path, and a way for an
artist to know who their audience actually is. It is on this site for one
reason, and it is not the music. It is that the thing underneath it is the same
machine as the clinic funnel, pointed at a completely different industry — and
that is the claim a portfolio of one vertical cannot make.

Front end is Next.js with Supabase. The interesting part is what is behind it.

## The same spine

GoHighLevel is not a bolt-on here; it is the spine. Artist pages are built in
the GHL builder. Merch runs through a GHL modal sitting over Shopify, so the
commerce is Shopify's problem and the customer is GHL's. Thank-yous, customer
capture, abandoned carts and segmentation are all handled there rather than
reimplemented in the app.

The Fan Connect module is a lead-capture pipeline wearing different clothes. A
fan signing up to hear from an artist and a lead asking about a clinic are the
same event: capture, validate, tag, segment, and then message the person on a
schedule that respects who they are and where they are. Six third-party APIs are
orchestrated behind it.

## The part that generalises

Consent is where this earns its place next to the timezone work.

Signl has to satisfy GDPR and California privacy rules from a single
implementation. The naive build forks: a European code path, a Californian one,
and a default. That fork is a maintenance liability — every subsequent feature
has to be written three times and will eventually be written correctly twice.

The approach is the same one the [scheduling
engine](/systems/scheduling-timezone) uses on time. Resolve the visitor's
jurisdiction once, at the edge of the system, into a small set of facts about
what is permitted. Then let one code path read those facts. It is the same
shape: a hard problem about *where somebody is* answered once, in one place, so
that nothing downstream has to know it was ever hard.

> Clinics, med spas, insurance — and a music network. Either the architecture
> generalises or it does not, and this is the evidence that it does.
