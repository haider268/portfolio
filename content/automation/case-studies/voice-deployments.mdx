Not one deployment — a practice. Production voice agents answering real
callers for healthcare clinics, insurance brokerages, med spas, and golf
academies, several of them multi-location and spanning timezones. Every
client is anonymised here; the mechanics are not.

## Owned end to end

- **Conversation behaviour.** Flow, turn-taking, interruption handling,
  tool calls, and human handoff — the full
  [agent surface](/systems/voice-agents), replicating each client's real
  qualification, booking, and escalation logic.
- **Telephony that respects the humans.** Conditional routing rings the
  desk phone first; only an unanswered call fails over to the agent, and
  rule-based transfers hand a live caller back to a person when the
  conversation calls for it.
- **A tuned speech pipeline.** Deepgram model, endpointing and
  sensitivity settings evaluated against real call recordings — the
  latency-versus-quality tradeoff [retuned per deployment](/systems/call-qa),
  not defaulted.
- **Post-call pipelines.** Summaries, outcomes, recordings and contact
  updates land automatically on the correct CRM record over webhooks —
  the office opens in the morning to a system that already filed its
  own paperwork.

## Why it holds up

Every deployment carries the same [reliability floor](/systems/reliability):
webhook orchestration, logging, monitoring, retry handling, and failure
recovery — and live call issues are flagged by the system before a client
reports them.
