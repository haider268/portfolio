The first call gets the attention. The follow-up gets the revenue: in the
[launch that filled an opening week](/work/wellness-launch), roughly
three quarters of conversions were driven by automated follow-up rather
than the first touch — with zero manual lead handling anywhere.

## What runs

- **Missed-call recovery.** A call that goes unanswered triggers an
  instant SMS with a booking link, so the lead's moment of intent is never
  wasted on a voicemail.
- **Nurture at every stage.** Sequences keyed to pipeline stage in the
  CRM, not one blast list — a lead that books leaves the nurture path
  automatically, a lead that stalls gets the next touch.
- **Database reactivation.** Multi-channel SMS and email loops run
  against dormant legacy lead bases — [gated strictly to each
  recipient's local business hours](/systems/scheduling-timezone), with
  STOP flipping do-not-disturb automatically.
- **Write-back.** Every outcome — booked, replied, opted out — lands in
  the CRM record, so the pipeline reporting is the system's own log,
  not a spreadsheet reconstructed after the fact.

## The engineering underneath

Event-driven, webhook-orchestrated, and built on the same
[middleware discipline](/systems/gtm-engineering) as everything else:
validation before any send, retry with backoff, and error recovery flows —
production automations holding above 99% run reliability.
